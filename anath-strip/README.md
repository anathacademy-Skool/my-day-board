# Anath AI buttons

The buttons above the typing box: ☰ Menu, ☀️ My day, 🎯 To-do, 📧 Email and 🏆 Winning ads, and on a second line
✨ What's new, 🎞️ Memories and 📅 Planner. In an Anath AI Academy student's My brain folder each one appears once its
lesson is done; in an Anath AI client's folder they're all on. Anywhere else it shows nothing.

This holds the design only: the layout, the button words and three tick sounds. No names, emails, to-dos, money or
any personal information. Everything a person sees comes from their own folder, on their own computer.
