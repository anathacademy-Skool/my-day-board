# Anath AI Academy buttons

The buttons above the typing box for Anath AI Academy students: ☰ Menu, ☀️ My day, 🎯 To-do, 📧 Email and
🏆 Winning ads. Each one appears once its lesson is done. It only shows inside a student's My brain folder.

This holds the design only: the layout, the button words and three tick sounds. No names, emails, to-dos, money or
any personal information. Everything a student sees comes from their own folder, on their own computer.
